Teachers · approving a pick-up

Room ____________

Someone has come to collect a child and isn't on their list. Nearly always it's a grandmother, an auntie or a family friend, and all is well.

Three things worth checking

  1. Does the child know them? Ask the child, not the adult.
  2. Can you reach a parent? Numbers are on the screen. Thirty seconds usually settles it.
  3. Does anything feel off? An unusual hurry, or a story that shifts. Worth paying attention to.
It's completely fine to ask someone to wait. "Let me give Mum a quick ring, I won't be a minute." Nobody here will think twice about it.

If you're happy

  1. Tap Someone else is picking up
  2. Add their name
  3. Write what happened, in a few real words
"Mum rang ahead at 10:15. Adam's auntie, Linh knows her, she had the collection tag."
Your name and the time are saved with it, and it can't be edited later. That's what makes it worth having.
If you'd rather not, then don't. Keep the child with you, stay friendly, ring the parent. If nobody answers, find Ate or a pastor. Twenty extra minutes in a warm room is a very small thing.
