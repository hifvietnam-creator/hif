# KidzQuest guides

Four files. Open in Chrome, **Ctrl+P → Save as PDF**.

| File | For | Print as |
|---|---|---|
| `manual-ta.html` | Helpers (teaching assistants) | A4, double-sided |
| `manual-teacher.html` | Teachers | A4, double-sided |
| `manual-admin.html` | Ate | A4, double-sided |
| `quick-card.html` | Every room | A4, cut in half, laminate |

The quick card is the one that gets used. Two A5 cards: one for everybody, one for
teachers. The guides are read once at home, the card lives on the table.

---

## No screenshots needed

Every screen is drawn into the page, with red numbered circles pointing at the exact
button. Nothing to capture, and no real children's names in a document that gets
printed and left on tables.

If a screen changes in the app, the drawing here needs changing too. They're kept
next to the code for that reason.

---

## Before handing them out

- [ ] Fill in the phone numbers at the bottom of each file. They print as blank lines
      if you'd rather write them by hand.
- [ ] Read the helper's guide yourself. If a step doesn't match what you see on the
      screen, the app has moved on and the guide needs updating.
- [ ] One card per room, laminated.
- [ ] Check the teacher's guide with whoever has done the most Sundays. They'll know
      what actually confuses people.

---

## What's in each

**Helper's guide** — signing in, finding your room, the three numbers, checking a child
in, the two tags, adding a child who isn't on the list, sending children home, and what
to do when the wifi drops. Nine steps.

**Teacher's guide** — the same, then a longer section on approving someone new to
collect: three things worth checking, how to word the note, and permission to say no.

**Ate's guide** — accounts and roles, putting people in rooms each week, editing the
Kids table, how grades sort children into groups, who can collect whom, archiving a
child who has left, the dashboard, the August move-up, and what to do if something
breaks on a Sunday.

The admin guide names two jobs worth doing first: **26 children have nobody who can
collect them**, and **107 have no school grade**, which is what August needs.
