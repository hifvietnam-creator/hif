Running KidzQuest

Everything on the admin side, and the handful of jobs that come round each week.

You're the only person who can do any of this. Teachers and helpers see the check-in screen and nothing else, which keeps their Sunday simple and keeps the children's details in one pair of hands. Yours.

There are four screens down the left. Dashboard for how things are going, Kids for the children, and Teachers and Assistants for who's helping where.

Giving people access

1

Make an account

Go to /admin, then Users, then Create New.

You'll need their name, their email, and a temporary password to send them. Then pick their role on the right hand side.

AdministratorEverything. You, and anyone standing in for you.
TeacherCheck-in screen, and can approve a new person collecting.
Teaching assistantCheck-in screen for their own room.
Do give everyone a role. Without one they can sign in but every screen will be empty, which looks like the app is broken.

Ask them to change the password the first time they sign in.

Put people in rooms

Open Teachers or Assistants and tap the room chips.

Four little buttons next to each person, one per group. Tap to put them in, tap again to take them out.

This is the one that stops Sunday working if it's missed. Without a room, a helper signs in and every room tells them it isn't theirs.

A yellow line at the top tells you how many people still have nowhere to go.

The little green squares show the last eight Sundays each person served. Handy for spotting someone who's quietly stopped coming, or someone doing rather too much.

Editing anything

Open Kids and type straight into any box.

Changes save when you click away. There's no Save button and nothing to submit.

  • 1The buttons along the top are shortcuts to the children who need something
  • 2Type a school grade and the group sorts itself out
  • 3The little arrow opens the adults for that child
Press Escape while you're still in a box to undo. Once you click away it's saved, though every change is kept with your name on it, so nothing is ever really lost.

Who can collect a child

Tap the arrow at the start of a row.

Each adult has a May pick up tick box. Being a contact and being allowed to take a child home are two separate things, so a grandmother can be reachable without being on the list at the door.

+ Add an adult for anyone new. If their phone or email is already on another child, they'll be joined up rather than duplicated.

When a child stops coming

Tap Archive at the end of their row.

You'll be asked which of these it is, and the difference matters more than it looks:

Left HanoiFamily moved away. Nothing to follow up.
Stopped attendingStill in town. This is the one worth a phone call.
Moved to AftershockGrown out of KidzQuest. Not a loss.
DuplicateSame child twice. Keep the other one.

Nothing is deleted. Their attendance stays, and Restore brings them back into the group they were in if the family reappears.

Teachers and helpers can archive too, since they're usually the first to know a family has moved. You'll see it on the Left tab.

The job worth doing first

26 children have nobody who can collect them Roughly one in five

These came across from the spreadsheets with a first name and nothing else. No parent, no phone number. Several of them have been attending all term.

On a Sunday, each one needs a teacher to approve their going home. Twenty-six of those in one morning and everyone stops treating it as unusual, which is the one thing that would take the value out of the whole system.

It's phone calls, not computer work. Open Kids, tap No collector, and you have your list. A volunteer with a phone could clear most of it in an hour.

For each one: expand the row, + Add an adult, name and number, tick May pick up. Done.

The dashboard

Four numbers at the top, then attendance by group, recent Sundays, and a Needs a look list at the bottom.

The Roster column on recent Sundays is how many children were on the register that week, not today. So a Sunday in May shows May's numbers even though children have joined since.

Something not seen for a few weeks isn't always a problem. Families travel, especially over Tet and the summer. The list is there to prompt a thought, not to make anyone chase.

Every August

KidzQuest moves everyone up on the first Sunday of August. Not everyone moves room, which is why the grades matter. Voyagers holds grades 1 and 2, so only the grade 2s go up to Trailblazers and the grade 1s stay where they are.

Grade 7s finish KidzQuest and go to Aftershock. Nobody is moved there automatically, because that's a conversation with a family rather than something the app should decide.

This one needs the person who looks after the website. It isn't a button yet. Give them a few weeks' notice before August rather than the week before.

If something breaks on a Sunday

Everything lives in the database, and that can be read even if every tablet in the building has died. This answers "who is still in the rooms right now":

select c.first_name, c.last_name, a.status,
       a.checked_in_at, a.checked_out_at
  from kq.attendance a
  join kq.children c on c.id = a.child_id
  join kq.sessions s on s.id = a.session_id
 where s.service_date = current_date;

Worth having open in a tab on a Sunday morning. If you'd rather not touch it, whoever looks after the website can run it in a moment.
