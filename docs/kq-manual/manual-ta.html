Your Sunday with KidzQuest

A short guide to the tablet. About ten minutes to read.

Thank you for helping with KidzQuest. Giving up your Sunday morning for these children matters more than you probably realise. This guide walks through the tablet you'll use at the door. There's also a card in your room with the short version, so you won't need to carry any of this in your head.

The paper register told us that a child had come. The tablet does that too, and it also keeps a note of who took them home. So if a parent ever asks where their child is, we can answer straight away.

The two tags

Two tags print with the same code on both.

  • 1One goes on the child. It has their name, their group and any allergy.
  • 2The other goes to the adult, who brings it back at the end.

Matching the two codes is a quick way to find the right adult in a busy room.

The tablet is what tells you who is allowed to take a child home. If someone has a matching tag but their name isn't on the list, please still check with a teacher first.

A child who isn't on the list

Scroll to the bottom and tap Add a child who isn't on the list.

This happens fairly often. New families join us, and children move up between groups.

You'll need their name, the adult who brought them, a phone number, and any allergies. Please do ask about allergies rather than assuming there aren't any.

Whoever you write down here is the person who can collect this child today, so it's worth taking a second over the name.

Ate fills in the rest during the week, so there's no need to ask for birthdays, school years or email addresses.

9

Someone new is collecting

Go and grab one of the teachers. They can approve it.

Two people having a look is simply safer, and it makes a mix-up far less likely. That's the only reason this one isn't yours to do.

Most of the time it turns out to be a grandparent or a family friend and all is well. Something like this keeps it easy:

"Let me just grab one of our teachers. We always check when someone new is collecting. Won't be a moment."

If you ever feel unsure about anything, take your time. Nobody here will mind waiting.

How it looks on your screen

If something goes wrong

The wifi drops out

Carry on tapping. Nothing gets lost.

The little dot at the bottom of the screen tells you what's happening.

GreenEverything is saved
AmberA few taps are waiting. Carry on.
GreyNo connection, but still fine to tap
While the dot is amber, please don't close the tab or refresh the page. Anything still waiting would be lost.
