# hif.vn sermon series — complete index

74 posts in `category/sermons` (id 51), enumerated across 8 archive pages.
The total matches the category's own count, so this list is complete.
**Read-only. Nothing written to Payload.**

Every post is one series; individual sermons live *inside* the post as repeated
blocks. So 74 posts ≈ 600+ individual sermons.

---

## The finding that matters most

**The current parser works on 10 of these 74 posts.**

Sermon dates inside the posts only include a year from **REMARKABLE (Jan 2025)**
onward. Everything older is written `| November 24` or `– Dec 25` with no year,
so the date-anchor regex finds nothing and the post yields zero entries.

There's a second, harder wall: posts before ~2018 have **no YouTube links at
all** — they're MP3-only. Since the video ID is the join key to Payload, those
14 posts cannot be matched by any current mechanism.

| Era | Posts | Video | Resource labels | Parseable now |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A — dated, YouTube + OneDrive | 10 | `youtu.be/<id>` | Download sermon slides · Discussion Group Guide · 5-Day Devotional Guide | **yes** |
| B — undated, YouTube + OneDrive | 22 | `youtu.be/<id>` | Download sermon slides (+ Discussion/Devotional from early 2024) | no — year missing |
| C — undated, YouTube + PowerPoint | 28 | `youtu.be/<id>` | Sermon PowerPoint Here · Download Sermon PowerPoint File Here · 40 Days Prayer Booklet · Bible Study Guide · Kids/Youth Sermon Guide | no — year missing, labels unrecognised |
| D — MP3 only | 14 | **none** | PLAY MP3 · PPT Sermon File | no — unmatchable |

The year is recoverable for eras B and C: each post carries a WordPress
publication date, and the sermons run forward in sequence from it. Series that
straddle New Year (Christmas ones especially) need care, but it's tractable.

## Things that will need decisions

**Two campuses, two sermons per Sunday.** From 2019 back, most Sundays have a
MyDinh sermon *and* a Westlake sermon — different speakers, same date. The
Sermons collection has no campus field, so these would collide.

**Duplicate series names.** `GOD WITH US` appears twice (2023 and 2024),
`CHARACTER COUNTS` twice (2019 and 2024), `JONAH` twice (2019 and 2025),
`The Blessed Life` twice (2019 and 2023). Matching a Payload Series by name
alone will pick the wrong one.

**`THRONE OF GRACE` looks empty.** It's the only post with no excerpt at all.

**A typo in Christmas 2025**: one entry reads `January 04 , 202` — truncated
year plus a stray space. It will not parse under any rule.

**Older posts have resource types with nowhere to go**: 40 Days Prayer Booklet,
Bible Study Guide, Kids Sermon Guide, Youth Sermon Guide, List of Lament Psalms.
Only slides and discussion guide have fields today.
