What a TA does

  • Tap a child → choose who dropped them off → label prints.
  • Switch to Check out to release them. Only adults marked as authorised appear.
  • An adult who isn't on the list can still collect — but it needs a teacher and a typed reason, and it is recorded.
  • The counter at the top never lets "who is still here?" be a guess.

What it does when the wifi drops

  • The roster is already on the device. Taps keep working.
  • The dot at the bottom turns amber and says how many are queued.
  • Labels still print — printing never waits on the network.
Open question for Sunday. If a tablet dies with children checked in, the room has no record at all. One printed roster per room, as a fallback, costs nothing.
